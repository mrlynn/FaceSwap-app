FaceSwap in 25 seconds.

Swap a face, hide one three ways, pick from the gallery, and borrow a face from the call.

Five styles

Hide your face, or wear another one.

Choose a style from the dock at the bottom of the window. Blur, Pixelate and Blackout work as soon as the app is installed. Swap and the touch-ups use separately licensed models (more on that).

The face gallery

A shelf of faces, ready to wear.

FaceSwap comes with a gallery of generated portraits. Click one to wear it, press 1–9 to switch, or roll the dice for a mix of two or three faces that belongs to nobody. You can also add your own folder of portraits.

FaceSwap Camera

Works in every meeting app, with no plug-ins.

FaceSwap installs a virtual camera on your Mac. In Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Teams, Slack or a browser, choose FaceSwap Camera. The other people on the call see whatever FaceSwap shows.

  • Change styles, faces and looks during a call, from the app, the menu bar or a global shortcut.
  • Close the window and FaceSwap keeps running in the menu bar, still feeding the camera.
  • Optionally mark swapped video with an “Altered” label, so you can use a swap openly.

Borrow a face Click the face to wear.
A borrowed face is a loan: it can’t be saved or shared.
  • One picture, then gone. FaceSwap reads the faces from a single screenshot of the window you chose and drops it straight away. Nothing is watched in the background.
  • A loan, not a keepsake. The face lasts until you pick another or quit. It can’t be saved to your gallery or kept as a look.
  • Always marked. While you wear a borrowed face, your picture carries an “Altered” label, whatever your settings say.

Borrowing uses Swap, so it needs the separately licensed Swap models. How it works →

Built for calls

More than a mask.

FaceSwap also covers the awkward moments of a video call.

Quick cards

Put a message in your picture: “Back in 5” with a countdown, “Lost my audio”, “Listening, can’t talk”, or your own words. ⌃⌥⌘K

Follow Me

The picture crops and pans gently to keep you centred as you lean, stand or move around. ⌃⌥⌘F

Presenter Mode

Choose a window and it becomes your picture, with you in a corner tile or cut out in front of it. It works in any meeting app. ⌃⌥⌘P

Hand-sign reactions

A thumbs-up sends thumbs floating up the picture, two fingers drops confetti, and an open hand raises a badge. Everyone on the call sees them.

Away card and privacy

Step away and a blurred card says you’ll be back. Hide Other People blurs anyone who walks into the picture behind you.

Clips

Record up to 30 seconds with faces already hidden or swapped, with sound, and share it. An optional before/after wipe shows the reveal. ⇧⌘R

Looks and scenes

Save a face with its mix, lighting and background as a look, and share it as a file. Scenes like Film Noir, Seventies and Studio change everything in one click.

Backgrounds

Blur the room, replace it with your own picture, a plain colour or a green screen, and add picture styles: Noir, Vintage, Cool, Vivid, Faded.

Private by design

Detection, swapping and touch-ups all run on your Mac’s GPU and Neural Engine. There’s no account and no cloud processing.

The Adjust card

Fine-tune it while you watch.

Press ⌘I and a small card opens over the picture, so you can see each change as you make it. It has four pages:

  • Face: your photo, a portraits folder, and Mix, which slides between your face and the one you’re wearing.
  • Look: lighting, smoother skin, hair and lip colour, whiter teeth, brighter eyes.
  • Background: the room, your picture, a colour or a green screen, plus scenes.
  • Speed: face detail from Off to Best, with live frame rate and delay.
Mix

A face that belongs to nobody.

Blend a second gallery face into the one you’re wearing, or set Strength below 100% to keep some of your own face. Mixing only changes the identity the model is given, so it doesn’t slow anything down.
